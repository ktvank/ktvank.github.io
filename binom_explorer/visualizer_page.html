Historical Binomial Visualizer

This tool tracks how often word pairs appear in alphabetical versus reverse-alphabetical order across historical American English text — a measure called ordinality. Shifts in ordinality over time can reflect changes in language, culture, or social context.

Enter one or more word pairs below to explore their history. Pairs are automatically sorted alphabetically, so left right and right left are treated the same.
