I am a postdoctoral researcher at the [Johns Hopkins Data Science and AI Institute](https://ai.jhu.edu/), working with [Anjalie Field](https://engineering.jhu.edu/faculty/anjalie-field/) in the [Field Lab](https://anjalief.github.io/). My research examines how large language models interact with human language: how sociolinguistic variation in prompts systematically shapes LLM outputs, why LLMs exhibit distinctly non-random behavioral patterns, and how people communicate with and through AI systems. I approach these questions using computational linguistics, network science, and large-scale data analysis. I received my PhD in 2025 from Cornell University, advised by [Jon Kleinberg](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/home/kleinber/).

**Research interests:** AI linguistics &nbsp;·&nbsp; LLM behavior &nbsp;·&nbsp; human/AI communication &nbsp;·&nbsp; language variation &nbsp;·&nbsp; computational linguistics &nbsp;·&nbsp; network science
