Large language models are trained on human language — all of it. That means they absorb not just our vocabulary and grammar, but our biases, our behavioral quirks, our social patterns. I study what that means. I'm a postdoctoral researcher at the [Johns Hopkins Data Science and AI Institute](https://ai.jhu.edu/), working with [Anjalie Field](https://engineering.jhu.edu/faculty/anjalie-field/) in the [Field Lab](https://anjalief.github.io/). My research examines how sociolinguistic variation in prompts shapes LLM outputs, why AI systems exhibit distinctly human behavioral patterns, and how humans and AI systems adapt to each other over time. I received my PhD in 2025 from Cornell University, advised by [Jon Kleinberg](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/home/kleinber/).

**Research interests:** AI linguistics &nbsp;·&nbsp; LLM behavior &nbsp;·&nbsp; human/AI communication &nbsp;·&nbsp; human-AI co-adaptation &nbsp;·&nbsp; language variation &nbsp;·&nbsp; computational linguistics &nbsp;·&nbsp; network science
