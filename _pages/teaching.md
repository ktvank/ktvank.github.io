## Teaching

In Spring 2023, I co-taught **CS 4820: Advanced Algorithms** at Cornell as an instructor with [Eshan Chattopadhyay](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/~eshan/). ([syllabus](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/courses/cs4820/2023sp/syllabus/)) The course enrolled roughly 450 undergraduate seniors and is a required course for CS majors. I have also guest-lectured in Jon Kleinberg's **CS 6850: The Structure of Information Networks**.

## AI in the News

I organize a weekly **AI in the News** reading group at Johns Hopkins, bringing together researchers and students to discuss current events at the intersection of AI and society — legal developments, policy, viral moments, social implications, and more. The goal is to build a shared understanding of the rapidly shifting landscape around AI outside of any single research lens.

If you'd like to join or suggest a topic, feel free to [get in touch](mailto:kvankoe1@jh.edu).
